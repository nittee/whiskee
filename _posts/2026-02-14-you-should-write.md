*A letter to oneself.*

<br>

They said that one should write. I remember my teachers that gave assignments that forced us, their students, to write essays. During those times, I really didn't understand why I should research a topic that I am not interested in, develop an idea from tens of articles about the given topic, and write a thousand-word essay. However, now that I am old enough to think that I am still young, after watching YouTube videos and reading online articles stating that one should write, I am beginning to understand the purpose of why writing is an important fact of life that everybody should try to do, whether it is good or bad writing, a sentence- or pages-long, and whether the idea is consistent or a jumbled mess. Everybody should share their thoughts, in a piece of paper, on your mobile note-taking app, or online (carefully and respectfully). In this way, we can understand ourselves, the way we think, and hopefully, improve ourselves. 

Why should one write? 

One should write because it is free.  
One should write because it provides structure to messy thoughts.  
One should write because it makes you think. 

Writing is free. Almost everyone has a smartphone to take notes. Even without a pen or paper, we can find ways to write. My mother once told a story about how they play a physical game called tubigan, also known as patintero. However, instead of using chalk to mark where the ‘it’ can step, they are using water to mark the lines. It is proof that there are ways to mark or write our ideas. It won’t be permanent, yes, but one can write. You can still express your ideas in a way for others to understand or visualize your thoughts. That’s a step for others to understand you, isn’t it? At the same time, it forces you to follow a structure that others will understand. Following a structure to be understood is necessary for someone to grab and capture your idea easily. 

One should write to be able to think more clearly. Writing forces our brain to organize our thoughts in a structured way, in a comprehensible and digestible way. But why would I want to understand my thoughts? Now you’re asking questions! And you’ve answered your own question already! One should write in a structured way to understand their own thoughts more clearly! And once you start writing freely and in a structured and comprehensive manner, you will begin to understand yourself and think more clearly and start asking questions you haven’t asked before!

You begin to think! You start thinking again! You start asking questions again! The blood flows to your brain again and you start creating, rather than mindlessly scrolling and consuming brainless contents. And you know that I know that you know that this is beneficial for your health. In the future, you will be more mindful, more creative, more expressive. You will feel yourself more (not in an ero way, hopefully). You will understand yourself more. You will share more, you can explain ideas much clearer. You can even have a friend, or rather, have someone more than friends, ha! These will all be possible since you are articulate. Big words, right? Anything can happen when you start writing. You don’t need to limit yourself. There’s no limit when writing! And that’s why You Should Write! 

Please. Write.
